Privacy and Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

1. Data controller

The controller of your data is Francisco Molina Ordóñez, holder of Spanish tax ID (NIF) 30958357N and based in Córdoba, Spain, Calle Cáñamo 6. For any question about your data you can write to info@lidrift.com.

2. What data we collect and why

The only form on the site is the free trial request form. It collects your name, your email address, your company (optional), the approximate size of the project and the message you write.

We use this data only to respond to your request and, where applicable, to prepare the free trial. We do not use it to send you advertising and we do not share it with third parties for their own purposes. We do not make automated decisions with it.

3. Legal basis

Processing is based on your consent, which you give by ticking the box on the form before sending it (Article 6.1.a of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time by writing to info@lidrift.com, without affecting the lawfulness of the processing carried out before.

4. How long we keep your data

We keep your data for as long as necessary to handle your request and, afterwards, for 30 days, or until you ask for its deletion. If the license is eventually purchased, the data will be processed under that contractual relationship.

5. Who your data is shared with

To provide the service we rely on providers that act as data processors:

  • Web3Forms: a service that receives the form submission and forwards it to our email.
  • Email provider: Porkbun (domain email forwarding) and Microsoft (Outlook), where we receive and store your message.
  • GitHub (GitHub Pages): hosts this website and, for technical security and operational reasons, may log the IP address of visitors.

Apart from these providers, we will only disclose your data if required by law.

6. International transfers

Some of these providers process data outside the European Economic Area, in particular in the United States and, in the case of Web3Forms, in India. These are the safeguards that, according to their own official documents, each one applies:

  • Web3Forms (Web3Creative): operates from India and may process data in other countries, including through the infrastructure regions of its sub-processors (such as Amazon Web Services or Cloudflare). For transfers from the European Economic Area, its data processing agreement incorporates the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914).
  • Microsoft (Outlook): may store and process data in your region, in the United States and in other countries where it operates. According to its privacy statement, Microsoft Corporation participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (and its UK and Swiss extensions) and also relies on the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.
  • Porkbun: Porkbun LLC processes data in the United States. Its privacy policy states that, for transfers, it applies appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses, or relies on an adequacy decision or on derogations provided for specific situations. It has designated a representative in the European Union.
  • GitHub (GitHub Pages): according to its trust center, for transfers from the European Economic Area it relies on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and on standard contractual clauses.

You can request more information about these safeguards by writing to info@lidrift.com.

7. Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and portability by writing to info@lidrift.com, stating which right you wish to exercise. We may ask you to verify your identity.

If you believe we are not processing your data in accordance with the regulations, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

8. Cookies

This site does not use first-party or third-party cookies for analytics or advertising purposes, which is why it does not show a cookie notice. Fonts are served from this same site, without connecting to third-party servers.

If an analytics tool or any other tool that uses cookies is added in the future, this page will be updated and your consent will be requested before it is activated.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect legal or service changes. The date of the last update appears at the top of the page.
