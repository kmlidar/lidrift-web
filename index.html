Frequently asked questions

What people usually ask before trying LiDrift on their own data.

What is LiDAR strip adjustment?

It means correcting the small position and orientation errors of each strip in a flight so that the overlap areas match vertically and horizontally. LiDrift does it using the terrain and its features as a reference: roofs, embankments and other structures.

Do I need ground control points (GCPs)?

No. LiDrift adjusts the strips to one another without control points. If you have them, it accepts them for absolute anchoring, and the report then adds the vertical accuracy (mean error and RMSE against the control points).

Does it work for drone, plane and helicopter flights?

Yes. LiDrift adjusts the strips from any platform, as long as you have the LAS or LAZ files of the flight and their trajectories.

What data do I need to get started?

The LAS or LAZ files of the flight and the trajectories of the platform (plane, helicopter or drone), each in its own folder, plus an output folder. The coordinate system and the height type are read from the file header.

Do I need to classify the point cloud or set up projects first?

No. There is no need to create projects, classify the point cloud or write a single line of code: you choose the three folders in a graphical interface and LiDrift does the rest.

What does the quality report include?

For each zone: the consistency between strips before and after adjustment, the overlap coverage and the number of strips, the pulse density and, if you use GCPs, the vertical accuracy. It also includes the flight data: total length, strips and total points processed. If you specify a work area, it adds the coverage and density recorded over it. It also documents the corrections applied to each strip.

Is my data sent to any server?

No. Processing is 100% local: LiDrift does not send your data anywhere.

Can I process several projects or zones at once?

Yes. LiDrift detects blocks that are kilometers apart within a single flight and adjusts each zone separately, with one report per zone. It can also chain dozens of projects in a single run.

How much does it cost, and is there a free trial?

The license is annual and per seat: €800 per year as the launch price and €1,500 per year afterwards, VAT not included. You can request a free trial on your own data before you decide.
